In addition to my work on entropy, I have also written about science and American art, measuring freedom, and the intuition of observational astronomers. I've published some of this work in peer-reviewed journal articles:

(2024) "From the Model to the Glance: How Astronomers Learned to See Gravitational Lenses, 1960-2020," in Historical Studies in the Natural Sciences

as well as shorter publications and essays:

(2026) "Reality as They Made It," in Whole Earth Redux, ed. Hannah Scott
(2025) "Learning to See Gravitational Lenses," in Physics Today

My earlier, scientific work used high-spatial-resolution and time-resolved Angle-Resolved Photoemission spectroscopy to study the properties of the MnBi2nTe3n+1 family of materials. You can find this stuff in Nature Physics, Physical Review B, and elsewhere.
